## Introduction

MyWalkup is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when using the mobile application.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
- Phone number for authentication and verification
- First and last name for your profile
- Optional profile photo/avatar image

**Team and Player Data**
- Team names and information you create
- Player names, jersey numbers, and song preferences
- Audio settings and voice preferences

**Usage Data**
- App interaction data and feature usage
- Device information (type, operating system)
- Crash reports and performance data

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the service
- Authenticate your account
- Process subscriptions and payments
- Send service-related notifications
- Improve the app and develop new features
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

## Third-Party Services

- **Firebase (Google)** - Analytics, crash reports, and push notifications
- **RevenueCat** - Manages in-app subscriptions
- **Apple** - Processes in-app purchases through App Store

## Data Storage and Security

Data is stored securely with appropriate technical and organizational measures protecting against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## Data Retention

Personal information is retained while your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request account and data deletion anytime through app settings.

## Your Rights

- Access copies of your personal data
- Update or correct your information
- Delete your account and associated data
- Request data in a portable format

## Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from this age group.

## Changes to Policy

Updates may occur with notification via posting and updated date stamps.
